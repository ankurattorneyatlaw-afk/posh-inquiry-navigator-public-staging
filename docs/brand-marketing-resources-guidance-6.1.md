# Navigator unified journey and marketing review 6.1

## Home page content review

Audience: organisational decision-makers, HR and IC/LC teams. The previous home page mixed free individual guidance, planned-workspace descriptions, fictional interface samples and several competing calls to action. It did not clearly separate the implemented procedural guide from proposed extended capabilities.

The new opening is “Run a fair POSH inquiry. Keep the next step clear.” It explains the currently implemented receipt-to-implementation workflow and makes Explore inquiry plans and See the guided process the primary choices. Subsequent sections connect document prompts, date review and handover to reduced avoidable rework and clearer preparation. A separate fair-process section addresses notice, participation, material and reasons as matters to handle during the inquiry. The closing invitation asks for a walkthrough and written scope, rather than implying immediate online purchase.

No appeal-proof claim, numerical cost/time saving, psychological-health promise, customer count or invented endorsement is used. Possible savings are qualified as dependent on actual use and process. Committee decisions, official signed records, browser/local-file recovery and the disabled real checkout are explained. Training feedback is not presented as proof of software outcomes. Extended planned capabilities remain subject to their stated scope on the plans page.

Free guidance, FAQs and the inquiry-stage overview remain reachable. The public header still has three links plus Sign in: Free guidance, FAQs and Resources. Inquiry plans remain prominent in home calls to action and the Resources page. Home meta descriptions and structured-data description were aligned with the implemented offering; the static CSP digest was updated for the changed structured data.

## Branding and account journey

The full POSH Inquiry Navigator name and The Cosmic Energizers attribution are consistent across home, dedicated login, customer workspace, administration and Resources. Login remains one email field with collapsed alternatives. Dashboards have a Home link, a clear page purpose and compact workspace navigation. Resources is linked from both dashboards, with distinct customer/admin explanations. Routing and permissions are unchanged: confirmed customer to /workspace, verified operator to /admin; no-plan customers cannot open paid inquiry assets and cannot open administration. Administrators retain access to implemented tools independent of customer subscriptions.

## Guidance readability and legal context

The former static sidebar repeated generic points and used 12–13px muted text. It is replaced by stage-specific references, a plain-language explanation and a practical next step. Sections 2(n)/3(2), 2(a)/2(o), 4/6, 9, 10–13, 19 and applicable Central Rule 7 are referenced only in the relevant stages. The legal wording was checked against the reproduced Act and Rules in NHRC annexures A–B: https://nhrc.nic.in/assets/uploads/training_projects/NHRC_Study_rpt_sexual_harrasemnt_workplaceDec2023.pdf . It does not make a finding or replace service-rule, forum or limitation review. The complaint window is expressed in calendar months; the applicable reply period in working days, triggered by receipt of prescribed documents.

Sidebar body is 18px at default reading size with higher contrast and 1.8 line spacing. The existing reading controls enlarge it. Stage labels, question hints, option descriptions and privacy copy were darkened after new public-guidance scans found contrast failures. Sources and limits remain collapsed. This changes presentation and explanatory context only, not legal routing, questions, answers, deadlines or Word documents.

## Resource catalogue

Added a public /resources.html catalogue for planned sample poster designs, a handbook and a POSH judgment-reference list. The three listings are explicitly Planned and have no invented price, file, preview or purchase button. Enquiry links do not send messages automatically. Customer dashboards state that purchased-file delivery is not enabled; the admin dashboard identifies the need for final files, previews, prices, editions and licence terms. No material entitlements, product table, file uploads, billing or real checkout were created. The existing test payment flow still covers inquiry plans only.
