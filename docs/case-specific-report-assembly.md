# Case-specific inquiry and review reports

The plan tools assemble a restricted, editable Word report from coded facts, procedural selections, the Committee’s recorded reasons and repeatable allegations, exhibits and witness statements. Essential, Professional and Advanced use the same report engine. Institutional and contractual offerings use the workspace features included in their access agreement. The guided inquiry’s Section 13 download uses the same engine and reuses timeline dates where available.

After initial plan access, report preparation and Word export run in the browser without network requests, an AI service or automatic saving. The 84-point review retains its 84 checks and eight gates. When used alongside the report in the plan tools, gate statuses and coded references carry into the report. Findings, factual assertions and sign-off come from the Committee record.

## Authority checked before encoding

- [POSH Act 2013, India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/19302/1/sexual_harrassment_of_women_at_workplace_act_2013.pdf). Sections 2(n) and 3(2) distinguish unwelcome conduct and circumstances connected with it. Sections 9–13 govern filing, settlement, procedure, interim measures, reports and action. Sections 14–18 govern the distinct false or malicious complaint pathway, compensation factors, confidentiality and appeal. Section 19 identifies employer duties.
- [POSH Rules 2013, Gazette G.S.R. 769(E)](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_CEN_13_14_00009_201314_1517807327213&filename=--------Rules%2C2013+09+Dec+2013.pdf&type=rule). The prescribed procedure includes Rule 7 participation and absence safeguards. Service and sector instruments can affect the governing procedure.
- The second proviso to Section 11(1) refers to both parties being employees. A different-employer record therefore calls for separate authority, coordination and service-rule particulars without automatically removing the representation safeguard. The domestic-worker referral is in Section 11(1). Section 11(2) deals with court compensation after conviction. The criminal provision and transitional basis are recorded as case-specific entries.

## Verification matrix

| Scenario | Expected report behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Section 2(n) only, and a second allegation with Section 3(2) | Separate conduct analysis for each. Connected-circumstance analysis appears only for the second. |
| Same employer, different employers, or only one party employed | Correct employer narrative and applicable representation language. |
| Respondent service rules, prescribed procedure, special instrument | Rule 7 text confined to the prescribed route. A distinct disciplinary record uses its recorded authority and disposition. |
| Domestic-worker referral, third-party referral, completed settlement, reported breach | Appropriate onward process. A subsisting settlement cannot produce a merits draft. |
| Multiple exhibits, including disputed and missing items | Stable IDs, restricted annexure entries, provenance, custody, objection or nonproduction details. Missing items are marked as having no enclosure. |
| Witnesses called by both parties and the Committee | Correct counts and one editable statement entry per witness. |
| Proved, not proved, partly proved or pending | Allegation-level reasons, relevant recommendation language and a pending-determinations entry where applicable. |
| Provisional findings, interim measures and Rule 7 absence decision | Provisional findings omit statutory final recommendations. Interim and absence records appear when selected. |
| Invalid date, inverted chronology, duplicate ID, dangling reference, incompatible route | Export stops and identifies the record to correct. |
| Editable Word export and coded audit | The exported document contains supplied facts and gate references in editable Word text and tables. |


## Report depth by plan

Every plan uses the same core statutory pathways, findings, annexures and safeguards. Higher plans add cumulative fields tied to the Committee's own record.

| Plan | Additional report detail |
| --- | --- |
| Essential | Core report with case facts, process, findings and annexures |
| Professional | Procedural decisions and material conflict analysis for each allegation |
| Advanced | Element-to-record reasoning, alternative explanations, procedural departures and applicable authorities |
| Institutional Plus | Committee constitution and independence, response to material objections, implementation authority and follow-up |
| Enterprise | Review issue-to-folio map, limits of each finding and preservation of the signed record |

The Committee enters authorities actually relied on, including the court, citation, paragraph and case-specific application. For procedural fairness and the context of departures under the CCS rules, see [Aureliano Fernandes v. State of Goa](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2012/21189/21189_2012_17_1501_44461_Judgement_12-May-2023.pdf). For external-member independence, see paragraph 22 of [Punjab and Sind Bank v. Durgesh Kuwar](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2019/16068/16068_2019_8_2_20978_Order_25-Feb-2020.pdf). A recorded authority or folio supports traceability. The Committee remains responsible for applicability, accuracy, reasons and approval.
