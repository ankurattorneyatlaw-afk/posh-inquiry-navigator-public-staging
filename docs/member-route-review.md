# IC and LC member route review

Live 5.2.0 review identified a wrong primary document for a respondent/employer-only conciliation request, a request question implying written-only eligibility, and an oral intake screen requesting written receipt prematurely.

The 5.2.1 correction distinguishes a verified request, an oral request still needing verification, no request, respondent-only request, unknown position, inquiry already commenced and unsuccessful discussion. The Committee route record is the primary document for unverified/no-request/no-agreement branches; it does not speak for the woman. A signed refusal is optional rather than a condition of ordinary progression. A verified request leads to the Committee facilitation response; the woman’s request form remains an optional companion, avoiding a needless fresh request. Respondent acceptance/decline forms are offered only after the woman’s request is verified. No-agreement does not generate a settlement or forwarding record.

Oral/unwritten intake displays assistance and owned follow-up instead of a missing-written-receipt prompt. The original oral approach can be recorded as a meeting/contact event, separate from written complaint receipt and inquiry commencement. Writing assistance does not acknowledge a written complaint or infer any dates. Pending/unknown branches retain owned next action and reassessment.

Primary legal verification: enacted Act, Annexure VIII, sections 9, 10 and 11, PDF pages 262–264 of the [Rajya Sabha compendium](https://cms.rajyasabha.nic.in/UploadedFiles/ElectronicPublications/SEXUAL_HARASMENT.pdf). Section 10 is before inquiry and on the aggrieved woman’s request; it does not expressly prescribe a written request, unlike section 12. Sector-specific applicable requirements still need competent review.
