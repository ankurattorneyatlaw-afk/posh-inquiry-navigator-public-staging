# Account onboarding and renewal operations

Navigator stores accounts and plan records only. Do not ask for complaint files, case references, evidence, payment credentials or OTPs in account support.

## New customer

Confirm the agreed current software scope, named buyer, plan, start/end boundary and fee externally. Create and confirm the customer's dedicated Navigator account using the approved authentication administration process. Only the designated owner may find that account and assign independently verified access. Confirm both the plan and exclusive end time; do not imply that licence seats create a shared case workspace.

Ask the member to use email-code sign-in privately. Their account screen provides a first-session checklist and workspace entry. Explain participant codes, institution-held source records and local recovery files. A recovery file contains recorded choices, document progress and timeline history; downloaded Word files and unrecorded fields must be retained separately. No case data is saved on Navigator servers or in web storage. Browser/device downloads may sync according to the user's own settings.

## Renewal

The account screen displays active plan expiry in IST and flags an end within 30 or seven days. The customer may download a calendar reminder or request renewal details. These are user-initiated actions, not automated email delivery or billing. No enquiry extends a plan.

Reply with current scope, fee/taxes, dates and commercial terms; independently verify any agreed payment. Use the owner's account lookup to review all access records before creating a renewal grant, avoiding unintended duplicate or overlapping entitlements. Record actual verified dates and the administrative verification reference. Refresh account access and confirm the new boundary. Do not revoke current paid access merely to create a future renewal.

When no active access is returned, it can mean missing/unverified access, a future start, expiry, revocation or a verification issue; do not label the cause without checking the account records. Knowledge-only access offers knowledge resources, not the inquiry workspace.

## Expiry or interrupted sign-in

Members keep the inquiry tab open. Its access-verification overlay offers a full recovery download, a new-tab sign-in link and a recheck. No case payload is sent by those account requests. Recheck only restores editing when active verified entitlement is confirmed. Access expiry does not delete the account or local files. Never promise a read-only case database or automatic recovery without a saved recovery file.

## Continuity and support

Use the dedicated Navigator project and free Render service, separate from POSH Universe. Explain that free hosting may cold-start. Keep an administrative renewal review schedule outside case records; do not claim a scheduled reminder service exists. Inspect delivery/provisioning failures without sharing secrets. Escalate security/account deletion issues through the approved provider process. Retain no customer passwords or sign-in codes.
