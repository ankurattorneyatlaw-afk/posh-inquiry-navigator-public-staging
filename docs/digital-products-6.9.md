# Digital products 6.9

The individual offer is self-service software, not consultations, appointments, personal document review or representation. The organisation workflow remains separate.

Catalogue: IC/LC Inquiry Checklist INR 999; Aggrieved Woman Advanced Guide INR 1,999 for 90 days; Respondent Advanced Guide INR 1,999 for 90 days. No automatic renewal. Displayed totals are catalogue prices. Every checkout is explicitly a zero-charge, non-binding test; no real invoice, subscription or purchase is created.

The checklist has eight pages and 42 checks, with a two-page public sample, statutory trigger reference, action register and official source links. Each guide has eight preparation stages, nine editable Word drafts, contextual guidance, personal review dates and local recovery. All case information must remain outside the account service. No narrative or evidence upload field or endpoint is added.

The shop validates the existing verified account, owns prices and product identity on the server, requires same-origin JSON and explicit current test terms, binds order confirmation to the creating account, handles expiry and duplicate confirmation, and rate-limits requests. Protected content requires the matching product entitlement. Orders and test entitlements are bounded in-memory records, reset by restart; the user is informed and may repeat test checkout. No change to Committee entitlements or account roles occurs.

Paid editions are authenticated encrypted packages. Neither plaintext product files, private authoring scripts nor the delivery key are published in the public repository. Delivery requires the private server key and verified product access. The public guide shell alone contains no paid wording.

Login preserves only three allowlisted product destinations. Unknown or external destinations cannot redirect a user outside Navigator. Existing customer/admin landing and role-assignment behaviour remains covered by its browser tests. Guide session loss retains browser progress and local export while stopping new draft actions. Recovery is strict, role-specific, locally read and confirmed before replacing progress; it does not restore product authorisation.

Validation: 52 build tests and 32 timeline/document tests; 65 new product browser/accessibility states across desktop and 320px layouts, no automated WCAG A/AA violations or horizontal overflow; all product checkout paths, protected PDF download, every individual-guide option, 18 actual Word downloads, both full recovery routes, wrong-role rejection, session holds and upstream answer correction. Separate browser checks cover all three product login returns, untrusted redirect rejection, existing customer/admin journeys and public catalogue/free guidance. PDF pages and all 18 Word drafts were rendered for visual review.

Statutory wording reviewed against Sections 9–18 of the Act reproduced in the official NHRC publication and the official Central Rules PDF. Section 11(4) does not expressly define commencement; first meeting is not substituted. Report and action triggers remain distinct. These are preparation aids, not certification or exhaustive current-law advice.

Before live sales: enable a verified live gateway only on explicit authorisation, durable account-bound product entitlements and reconciliation, purchase confirmation/invoices/tax treatment, refund handling and a completed live-sales operational review. No additional paid hosting resource is provisioned by this release.
