# Guided Inquiry timeline and document integration

This release extends the existing `/guided` P01–P47 workflow and its Section 10 completion route. It does not create a second inquiry app. Both final walkthrough routes require matching verified timeline records, and a settlement breach reopens review. Existing procedural exceptions and inline resolution tools remain present.

The timeline records complaint receipt, separately verified inquiry initiation and its source basis, first meeting, repeatable hearings and procedural events, inquiry completion, report provision, party availability, receipt of recommendations and implementation. It retains 90-day, 10-day and 60-day references with their distinct triggers. Rule 7 working-day references require applicability and a verified calendar. First meeting is never silently treated as initiation. A separate receipt-based monitoring safeguard flags initiation delay. Adjournment, interim relief and unresolved exceptions do not automatically pause or reset clocks.

Forty-seven editable Word drafts cover written-complaint format and writing assistance, acknowledgement and its separate internal control sheet, A-02 complaint registration and its separate administrative control sheet, conciliation information, clarification, respondent notice and reply, the woman’s conciliation request or decline, separate respondent and Committee responses, settlement and forwarding, breach intimation, hearing notice, attendance and quorum, minutes, adjournment, absence safeguards, evidence receipt and indexing, custody transfer, disclosure, witness invitation and statement, examination questions, evidence objections, interim relief, findings representations, report forwarding, implementation and records handover. Register drafts use native editable Word tables. Download requests are logged separately and do not mark a legal step issued, served or complete.

Documents appear beside the answer that makes them relevant. The following question stays hidden until the primary document download is started and the user explicitly chooses Move to next step. Written intake presents A-01 first, then the reviewed A-02 registration form, then A-03 case reference allocation and A-04 preliminary scrutiny, then the next intake question. Other optional drafts do not unlock the primary-document step. An oral or unwritten complaint shows a format and assistance record instead of falsely acknowledging written receipt. Related questions remain in the same page and group, but are revealed only after explicit continuation. P10A asks whether the woman has been informed about voluntary conciliation; P11 records her own request, refusal or uncertainty without presuming consent. In-place corrections invalidate dependent responses. The supplied A-01 and A-02 templates are adapted with IC/LC labels. Internal controls remain separate downloads. A-02 preserves the original complaint and true receipt date; it does not mandate complete intake fields or turn a preference into settlement consent. A-03 preserves neutral reference placeholders, restricted identity-index pointers and allocation checks. Allocation is administrative and does not initiate the inquiry; actual case numbers are not generated.

This remains a fictional staging workflow. It accepts no actual case identities, allegations, testimony or evidence. Records remain in tab memory until exported. There is no background notification delivery, authenticated institutional identity, shared case database or immutable audit storage. Drafts require independent procedural review, verified bracketed particulars and proper authorisation before any real-world use.

Legal review basis: Sections 9–13 and 16 of the POSH Act and applicable Central Rule 7, with service and sector-rule applicability explicitly subject to verification. Text inspected from India Code references and the Act and Rules reproduced in the NHRC published report annexures A and B. Section 11(4) gives the 90-day duration but does not expressly designate a first-meeting trigger; that date is therefore separate from verified initiation.

Validation: `npm run build` verifies signed static assets and the existing guided-flow regression suite, including closure gates. `node --test scripts/timeline.test.cjs scripts/workflow-documents.test.cjs` runs twenty-one checks of deadline triggers, calendar handling, audit correction, completion safeguards, contextual document selection, A-01/A-02/A-03/A-04 ordering and recipient/internal-record separation. `scripts/timeline-browser.cjs` checks both completion routes, breach review, draft downloads and export/restore. `scripts/document-progression-browser.cjs` verifies download-first progression, hidden next questions, A-01/A-02/A-03/A-04 ordering, unwritten intake, conciliation request/decline and contextual notices at desktop and mobile sizes. The former contextual-documents browser entry point delegates to this current regression suite. QA fixtures and renders stay outside `dist` and are not published.

A-04 is a restricted preliminary review draft, adapted from the supplied blank checklist. It preserves assistance, jurisdiction and constitution review, limitation and written extension reasoning, provisional allegation classification, safety, lawful service, action owner and follow-up, Committee directions and quality checks. IC Section 4 and LC Sections 6–7 composition are distinguished. Scrutiny dates do not set inquiry commencement or suspend/reset deadlines. Internal status does not create automatic legal privilege. Actual decisions and follow-ups require separate authorised recording; a download alone does not complete scrutiny.
All 20 declared exceptional destinations now retain an open procedural follow-up screen rather than an alternative completion message. Each provides issue-specific guidance, competent-recipient and legal-basis checks, controlled communication and receipt tracking, owned review dates and reassessment. Only C04 conciliation and P48 inquiry/report are principal completion destinations. P04 cross-organisation and visitor respondents both require competence review; the label alone does not decide jurisdiction or require LC referral. The referral shortcut prepares a planned timeline follow-up; the user must supply the actual event date, owner and review date and save it explicitly. No referral is sent and no deadline is paused. scripts/referral-browser.cjs covers both external relationships, follow-up recording, reassessment and verified continuation at desktop/mobile sizes; the guided model audit exercises every declared exceptional destination.

A-05 through A-08 are adapted from the supplied blank templates. A-05 appears at P08 and as an optional post-scrutiny communication; A-06 replaces the respondent-notice draft at P13, with separate recipient/internal exports. A-07 offers an optional request companion, respondent submission guide and restricted control sheet at notice/reply stages, without resetting the Rule 7(3) trigger or requiring a duplicate statement. A-08 is the primary P14 service record and is also offered at later relevant notice, disclosure and forwarding stages. It separates dispatch, actual prescribed-document receipt, incomplete delivery and re-service. These downloads do not issue, serve or adjudicate anything or automatically populate legal milestones. All eight Word exports were rendered and visually reviewed. scripts/notices-browser.cjs verifies recipient/internal separation, primary-download gating, optional A-07 and disputed-service review on desktop/mobile.
