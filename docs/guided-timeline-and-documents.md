# Guided Inquiry timeline and document integration

This release extends the existing `/guided` P01–P47 workflow and its Section 10 completion route. It does not create a second inquiry app. Both final walkthrough routes require matching verified timeline records, and a settlement breach reopens review. Existing procedural exceptions and inline resolution tools remain present.

The timeline records complaint receipt, separately verified inquiry initiation and its source basis, first meeting, repeatable hearings and procedural events, inquiry completion, report provision, party availability, receipt of recommendations and implementation. It retains 90-day, 10-day and 60-day references with their distinct triggers. Rule 7 working-day references require applicability and a verified calendar. First meeting is never silently treated as initiation. A separate receipt-based monitoring safeguard flags initiation delay. Adjournment, interim relief and unresolved exceptions do not automatically pause or reset clocks.

Thirty-six editable Word drafts cover written-complaint format and writing assistance, acknowledgement and its separate internal control sheet, conciliation information, clarification, respondent notice and reply, the woman’s conciliation request or decline, separate respondent and Committee responses, settlement and forwarding, breach intimation, hearing notice, attendance and quorum, minutes, adjournment, absence safeguards, evidence receipt and indexing, custody transfer, disclosure, witness invitation and statement, examination questions, evidence objections, interim relief, findings representations, report forwarding, implementation and records handover. Register drafts use native editable Word tables. Download requests are logged separately and do not mark a legal step issued, served or complete.

Documents now appear beside the answer that makes them relevant. Written intake immediately shows the recipient acknowledgement; an oral or unwritten complaint shows a format and assistance record instead. Related questions stay on the same screen within intake, conciliation, notice/reply, hearings/witnesses, evidence, reporting and implementation groups. P10A asks whether the woman has been informed about voluntary conciliation; P11 records her own request, refusal or uncertainty without presuming consent. Information and response-specific drafts stay alongside those answers. In-place corrections invalidate dependent responses. The recipient acknowledgement adopts the supplied template wording with IC/LC labels; the internal checklist and secure delivery-proof record remain a separate download.

This remains a fictional staging workflow. It accepts no actual case identities, allegations, testimony or evidence. Records remain in tab memory until exported. There is no background notification delivery, authenticated institutional identity, shared case database or immutable audit storage. Drafts require independent procedural review, verified bracketed particulars and proper authorisation before any real-world use.

Legal review basis: Sections 9–13 and 16 of the POSH Act and applicable Central Rule 7, with service and sector-rule applicability explicitly subject to verification. Text inspected from India Code references and the Act and Rules reproduced in the NHRC published report annexures A and B. Section 11(4) gives the 90-day duration but does not expressly designate a first-meeting trigger; that date is therefore separate from verified initiation.

Validation: `npm run build` verifies signed static assets and the existing guided-flow regression suite, including closure gates. `node --test scripts/timeline.test.cjs scripts/workflow-documents.test.cjs` runs fourteen checks of deadline triggers, calendar handling, audit correction, completion safeguards, contextual document selection and recipient/internal-record separation. `scripts/timeline-browser.cjs` checks both completion routes, breach review, draft downloads and export/restore. `scripts/contextual-documents-browser.cjs` checks immediate acknowledgement, unwritten intake, same-screen conciliation questions, answer corrections and contextual notice downloads at desktop and mobile sizes. QA fixtures and renders stay outside `dist` and are not published.
