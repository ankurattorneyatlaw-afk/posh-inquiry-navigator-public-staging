# Evidence references and report review 6.10

The existing Committee workspace now has a browser-only evidence and annexure index. A neutral label such as Chat extract or CCTV clip receives a stable proposed A-number. Corrections preserve history; withdrawn references remain reserved. Numbers are administrative proposals, not statutory exhibit marks, admissions, authentication or findings. The reviewer must check existing institutional markings and the actual record before adopting them.

The index records source/custody references, actual receipt or a recorded uncertainty review, issue codes, marking review, integrity/preservation review, disclosure or protective directions, response opportunity, objections, recorded reliance/non-reliance and actual attachment/location references. Only short codes and neutral labels belong here; evidence files, identities and testimony remain in institutional custody. No server case-storage endpoint or database is added.

Working inquiry-report, evidence-register and findings-matrix downloads append the current index and issue cross-references. Supporting and contrary material is retained in the reference trail; the app does not produce findings or decide weight. The standalone index and working report can be downloaded for review while gaps remain. Actual source, disclosure, opportunity, objection, analysis and attachment references are visible in the draft.

Material dated after recorded inquiry completion or report submission is flagged for competent chronology/further-procedure review; the app does not silently insert it into a concluded report, backdate records or reset a deadline. Recorded active items with unresolved checks keep inquiry-session completion at a review step with a direct route back to the item. Review reminders identify owner, date, overdue/due/approaching status and next checks. This is an administrative readiness control, not a statutory condition or time extension. The user is told not to delay a required report merely because a software reference field remains unresolved, and to obtain a competent direction promptly. Section 10 completion is not converted into an inquiry route by the index.

Full recovery is now version 3 and includes the index, its replayable correction history and unsubmitted evidence-form fields. Versions 1 and 2 remain importable with an empty evidence index; the replacement preview makes the count and replacement explicit. Version 3 prevents old clients from silently ignoring a new index. All restore components are prepared before replacement; inconsistent history is rejected and the prior session is retained. History is explicitly untrusted and is not a tamper-proof institutional audit log.

The evidence scripts have the existing server-side Committee licence checks. Individual product test access never grants access to them. Real payments remain disabled. Account services handle only account/access data; no evidence entry is sent to them.

Individual Word edition 1.2 also implements the requested 1.35 line spacing, justified body paragraphs, a recipient indent after To, left-aligned headings and structured fields, and clearly identified italic optional passages. Separate notes explain conditional use and removal. Required procedural wording is not styled as optional. Main templates and guides remain separate downloads.

Validation: 65 build tests and 32 timeline/document tests; desktop/mobile evidence workflow checks, actual report/library/index downloads, all 65 individual-product browser/accessibility states and 36 Word downloads, legacy imports, version 3 recovery, preservation of unsubmitted fields, atomic tamper rejection, existing IC/LC inquiry journeys and oral/unwritten complaint routes. The 36 individual documents and working evidence/report outputs are rendered for visual review. No automated accessibility violations or horizontal overflow were found in the tested new states.

Legal verification uses the Act reproduced in the official NHRC publication, especially Sections 11, 13 and 16, and the official Central Rules, especially Rule 7. This release does not assert that every service/sector procedure, judicial direction or electronic-record issue has been independently certified. It does not impose a universal electronic-certificate rule, infer authenticity from a fingerprint, or guarantee an appeal outcome. Committee decisions, source records and qualified legal review remain necessary where applicable.
